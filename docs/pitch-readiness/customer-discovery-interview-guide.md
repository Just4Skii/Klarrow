# Klarrow - Customer Discovery Interview Guide

**Target Interviewees:** Vice Presidents of Food Safety & Quality Assurance (VP FSQA), Regulatory Compliance Directors, Plant Quality Managers, Supply Chain Directors at food processing facilities (\$25M-\$500M annual revenue).  
**Methodology:** The "Mom Test" / Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD) principles. Avoid pitching features early; focus on concrete past recall events, mock drill frequency, document pain points, and existing tool budgets.

---

## 1. Interviewee Screening & Context (5 minutes)

1. *"Could you describe your facility's operations-what categories of food do you process, and how many raw ingredient suppliers do you interact with monthly?"*
2. *"What ERP or inventory management systems do you currently rely on across receipt, production, and distribution?"*
3. *"Under which regulations or audit standards do you operate (e.g. FSMA 204, GFSI, SQF, BRCGS, USDA FSIS)?"*

---

## 2. Past Incident & Mock Recall Drill Reality (15 minutes)

*Goal: Uncover the real workflow when an incident occurs, rather than the idealized SOP.*

4. *"Think back to the last mock recall drill or actual recall inquiry you conducted. Walk me through the first hour: who called, what information did you receive, and what did you open first on your computer?"*
5. *"How long did it actually take from notification to delivering the complete customer distribution list to the auditor or regulator?"*
6. *"Where did the process stall or get messy? Which records had to be hunted down manually across email threads or paper binders?"*
7. *"Have you ever encountered a situation where a supplier's lot number in your ERP didn't match the bill of lading or physical sack due to a typo or prefix difference? How was that caught?"*
8. *"What happens when a co-packer or external warehouse sends you a scanned PDF or photo of a dispatch slip instead of an electronic spreadsheet?"*

---

## 3. Mass Balance & Gaps in Traceability (10 minutes)

*Goal: Validate whether incomplete records and mass conservation discrepancies are an acute daily headache.*

9. *"During a mock recall, how do you verify mass balance (reconciling raw pounds purchased vs. finished goods shipped + waste)? What tool calculates that?"*
10. *"If a customer delivery slip or waste log is missing, how does your team document that gap without failing the GFSI/SQF audit?"*
11. *"How confident are you that an unaffected lot is never mistakenly included in a recall notice due to broad lot grouping?"*

---

## 4. Current Tooling & Evaluation of Existing Solutions (10 minutes)

12. *"What commercial traceability or recall software tools have you evaluated or deployed in the past (e.g. FoodLogiQ, iCiX, SAP GTS, TraceGains)?"*
13. *"If you chose not to purchase or stopped using them, what was the primary blocker? (e.g. supplier onboarding friction, cost, rigid data models, integration complexity)?"*
14. *"How much internal IT or consulting support was required to get your current traceability workflow functioning?"*

---

## 5. Value Perception & Budget Thresholds (10 minutes)

*Goal: Gauge commercial viability and willingness to pay based on concrete pain.*

15. *"If you could upload your raw supplier PDFs and production CSVs into an evidence reconciliation engine that reconstructs the trace graph and drafts an audit package with citations in 5 minutes, how would that impact your operational team?"*
16. *"Who in your organization owns the budget for food safety compliance tools? What is the typical authorization threshold for a specialized FSQA software license?"*
17. *"Would your management prefer an on-premise installation (to keep proprietary formulation batch records behind your firewall) or a secure cloud tenant?"*

---

## 6. Closing & Referral

18. *"If we provided a sandbox workspace with anonymized FDA recall scenarios for your quality team to test, would you be open to running a 30-minute side-by-side comparison against your current spreadsheet workflow?"*
19. *"Who else in your professional network is currently wrestling with FSMA 204 compliance deadlines that we should speak with?"*
